Engineered For Freedom

Every feature in Orion is crafted to protect your identity and accelerate your browsing experience.

🛡️

Tor-Inspired Privacy Shield

Dynamic 3-stage security slider (Standard, Safer, Safest) disabling scripts and WebGL on untrusted sites, with a one-tap "New Identity" incinerator.

🚫

Brave Shields Adblocking

Pre-network request drops evaluate queries in under 1 millisecond. Ad servers, DoubleClick, tracking pixels, and analytics are killed before connecting.

⚡

Firefox Fast Search & DoH

Encrypted Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) DNS-over-HTTPS bypasses slow ISP resolvers. Built-in Data Saver suppresses heavy images on metered cellular data.

🚀

Parrot 4x Video Downloader

Real-time media sniffer hooks HTML5 video tags, .mp4 files, and .m3u8 streams with 4-thread parallel chunk downloading and audio-only MP3 extraction.

🔒

Biometric Secret Vault

Keep your private tabs and downloaded media locked behind Android Fingerprint or iOS Face ID with AES-256 encrypted local storage.

🎵

Screen-Off Background Audio

Foreground media service keeps YouTube audio, podcasts, and streams playing seamlessly when switching apps or locking your phone screen.
